Career ArchitectureHow the Chart Is Wired

Layer 1 — Lagna Lord in H7

Jupiter (lagna lord) in Gemini H7. When the lagna lord sits in H7, the person's energy and identity flows toward others — partnerships, collaborations, public interaction, and one-on-one work. Jupiter in Gemini adds intellectual curiosity, communication agility, and a love of ideas and exploration. This is the archetype of someone who creates value through working with people and ideas, not executing within a hierarchy.

Jupiter in Gemini is comfortable with multiplicity — many interests, many connections, many threads. The career is unlikely to be a single straight-line corporate trajectory. It's woven from relationships.

Layer 2 — Saturn + Rahu in H6

Saturn in H6 Taurus (Rohini nakshatra) is the one factor that gives Ruhi more tolerance for structured environments than a pure Sagittarius chart. Saturn is comfortable in H6 (the service house) and can operate within discipline. Rohini nakshatra (ruled by Moon) adds a desire for growth, beauty, and creation in the work environment.

But Rahu beside Saturn introduces the disruptor. Rahu in H6 = unconventional, ambitious, restless in service environments. The employment track will feel increasingly wrong as Rahu amplifies the search for something more — foreign, unusual, or boundary-breaking. Rahu+Saturn H6 can bring competitive success in jobs but also sudden disruptions, dissatisfaction, or unconventional circumstances around employment.

Layer 3 — Moon in Swati H11

Moon at 7.1° Libra H11, Swati nakshatra (Rahu-ruled). Swati is the independent sapling bending in the wind — its power is in resilience and self-reliance. The deity is Vayu (wind god): freedom, movement, expansion. The shakti is "pradhvamsa" — scattering and reconverging like seeds in the wind.

Moon in Swati H11 means the emotional and income life is built through networks, diplomacy, and independence. Not corporate salary, but relationships that generate income organically over time. This is the influencer's placement — not the employee's.

Layer 4 — Ketu H12 and Depth Pull

Ketu at 29.6° Scorpio H12 (Jyeshtha nakshatra). Jyeshtha = "the eldest," ruled by Mercury, deity = Indra (king of the gods, wielder of lightning). Shakti: "arohana shakti" — power to rise. But Ketu here in H12 = the moksha house. This is a person with past-life depth and a natural pull toward wisdom, inner work, and letting go of surface ambitions. Any career that doesn't engage this depth will feel eventually hollow — no matter how prestigious the title.
